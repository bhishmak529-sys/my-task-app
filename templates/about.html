Welcome to TaskPro Elite

TaskPro Elite is a highly optimized, real-time productivity workspace designed for professionals and students. Engineered to streamline your workflow, it combines agile Kanban methodologies with seamless cloud synchronization, ensuring you never miss a deadline. Review the comprehensive guide below to maximize your efficiency.

📖 Comprehensive User Guide
🚀 1. Agile Kanban Workspace ⌄

The core of TaskPro Elite is built around an intuitive, drag-and-drop Kanban board.

  • ✓
    Task Lifecycle: New entries default to the Backlog. Click, hold, and drag cards across To Do, In Progress, and Done stages to reflect real-time status.
  • ✓
    Smart Filtering: Utilize the top navigation bar to instantly filter your workspace by Category (Work, Personal, Urgent) or Priority levels.
  • ✓
    Visual Indicators: Cards automatically highlight to visually warn you when critical deadlines are approaching.
📁 2. Task Architecture & Cloud Media ⌄

Attach vital resources directly to your workflows.

  • ✓
    File Attachments: Upload images, documents, and reference materials directly into task cards.
  • ✓
    Horizontal Media Gallery: View your task images seamlessly through an optimized, sideways-scrolling image gallery right inside the modal.
🤝 3. Real-Time Collaboration Ecosystem ⌄

Work seamlessly with your team using high-speed WebSockets.

  • ✓
    Live Task Movement: When a team member updates or moves a task, your screen syncs instantly without ever needing to refresh.
  • ✓
    Live Chat Room: Click on any task to enter its dedicated discussion room and chat instantly with collaborators to keep ideas flowing.
📊 4. Data Export & Reporting ⌄

Generate professional reports instantly for offline access or record-keeping.

  • ✓
    Dynamic PDF Export: Download a beautifully formatted, print-ready PDF report of your current board status with just one click.
  • ✓
    CSV Backup: Export all your tasks and descriptions into an Excel/CSV format for deep data analysis or safekeeping.
🔒 5. Advanced Security & Access ⌄

Your workflow data is protected with enterprise-level security measures.

  • ✓
    Google OAuth 2.0: Skip password creation entirely. Use secure, one-click sign-in via your existing Google Account.
  • ✓
    Encrypted Passwords: For standard accounts, all passwords are salted and hashed using modern PBKDF2 encryption before hitting the database.
✨ 6. Premium Aesthetic Optimization ⌄

A workspace should be a joy to look at. We crafted a UI that reduces eye strain.

  • ✓
    Muted Elegance Theme: Enjoy a sophisticated color palette designed to look professional, calm, and perfectly balanced.
  • ✓
    Creator's Calendar: View all your deadlines in a beautiful grid layout that syncs effortlessly with your main board.
