Welcome to TaskPro Elite

TaskPro Elite is a highly optimized, real-time productivity workspace designed for professionals and students. Engineered to streamline your workflow, it combines agile Kanban methodologies with seamless cloud synchronization, ensuring you never miss a deadline. Please review the comprehensive guide below to maximize your efficiency.

📖 Comprehensive User Guide

The core of TaskPro Elite is built around an intuitive, drag-and-drop Kanban board.
  • Task Lifecycle: New entries default to the Backlog. Click, hold, and drag cards across To Do, In Progress, and Done stages to reflect real-time status.
  • Smart Filtering: Utilize the top navigation bar to instantly filter your workspace by Category (Work, Personal, Urgent) or Priority levels.
  • Visual Indicators: Cards automatically highlight in red (Overdue) or amber (Due Soon) to ensure critical deadlines are met.

Clicking on any task card opens its dedicated detailed view, transforming a simple to-do item into a comprehensive project hub.
  • Descriptions & Notes: Add extensive details, URLs, or meeting notes to your task.
  • Sub-Task Delegation: Break complex tasks into manageable checklists. Monitor completion directly from the main card.
  • Cloudinary Integration: Securely upload project files, PDFs, and high-resolution images. All media is hosted dynamically on secure cloud servers.

TaskPro Elite eliminates the need for manual page refreshes during team efforts.
  • Instant Sharing: Invite colleagues via their registered username. The system automatically dispatches an encrypted email notification to them.
  • WebSocket Synchronization: When a collaborator moves a task or updates a status, the changes reflect on your screen instantaneously via Socket.IO technology.
  • Integrated Discussion Threads: Utilize the in-task chat interface to communicate contextually with your team regarding specific deliverables.

Maintain absolute control over your schedule and productivity metrics.
  • Data Dashboard: Access the Analytics section to view dynamically generated Chart.js graphs detailing your task distribution by status and priority.
  • Interactive Calendar: Switch to the Calendar View to manage monthly deliverables. Drag and drop tasks directly on the calendar grid to modify their deadlines.
  • Activity Audit Log: A tamper-proof history log records every action (edits, deletions, status changes) with precise timestamps for complete transparency.

  • Automated Morning Briefings: Our APScheduler background worker scans your database daily and delivers a personalized email summarizing your pending tasks for the day.
  • XP & Ranking System: Productivity is rewarded. Earn +10 XP for every task completed. Ascend through the ranks from Novice 🥉 to the prestigious Legend 💎 tier.
  • Data Portability: Export your entire workspace into a structured CSV format with a single click for local backups.
